ISNet "general use" ONNX weights, int8 weight-only QDQ quantization.

Files (a single 42.18 MB ONNX file split in three, joined in the browser, because
CDNs apply per-file size limits):
  isnet-general-int8.part1.onnx
  isnet-general-int8.part2.onnx
  isnet-general-int8.part3.onnx

Source: https://huggingface.co/xrds/isnet-general-onnx-int8 (onnx/model_quantized.onnx)
Base model: https://huggingface.co/imgly/isnet-general-onnx
Architecture: IS-Net, from "Highly Accurate Dichotomous Image Segmentation"
  (Qin et al., ECCV 2022), https://github.com/xuebinqin/DIS

Preprocessing: stretch to 1024x1024, then (pixel - 128) / 256 per channel.
Output: a single-channel sigmoid matte at 1024x1024.
